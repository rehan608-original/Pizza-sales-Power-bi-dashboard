# Executive Business Intelligence Dashboard: Pizza Sales Analysis

## Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze transactional sales performance and present actionable commercial insights[1][2]. By quantifying key financial metrics, evaluating purchasing trends across time, and identifying top and bottom menu items, the dashboard supports data-driven decisions regarding menu optimization, inventory allocation, and marketing strategies[1].

## Data Set Used

- <a href="https://github.com/rehan608-original/Pizza-sales-Power-bi-dashboard/blob/main/pizza_sales.csv">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)

* **What is the total revenue generated from pizza sales?**
* **What is the average order value per transaction?**
* **What is the total number of pizzas sold?**
* **How many total orders were placed?**
* **What is the average number of pizzas per order?**
* **Which days of the week experience the highest order volume?**
* **Which months generated the maximum total orders?**
* **What percentage of total sales is contributed by each pizza category?**
* **What percentage of total sales is generated by each pizza size?**
* **What is the total quantity of pizzas sold per category?**
* **Which are the top 5 and bottom 5 pizzas based on total revenue?**
* **Which are the top 5 and bottom 5 pizzas based on total quantity sold?**
* **Which are the top 5 and bottom 5 pizzas based on total order count?**

## Process

* **Data Ingestion:** Imported raw transactional pizza sales data into the SQL database.
* **SQL Querying &amp; KPI Extraction:** Wrote SQL queries to compute core KPIs including Total Revenue, Average Order Value, Total Pizzas Sold, Total Orders, and Average Pizzas Per Order.
* **Trend &amp; Breakdown Analysis:** Queried sales volume across days of the week and months, as well as percentage sales shares by pizza category and size.
* **Performance Ranking:** Applied SQL grouping and sorting to isolate the Top 5 and Bottom 5 menu items ranked by Total Revenue, Quantity Sold, and Total Orders.
* **Data Filtering Logic:** Developed conditional SQL `WHERE` clauses to segment data dynamically by specific pizza categories.
* **Dashboard Design (Power BI):** Designed an interactive dashboard containing dynamic summary cards, top/bottom seller visual bar charts, and category slicers.

## Project Insights

* **Overall Performance:** The restaurant generated **208.20K Total Revenue** across **9,085 Total Orders** and **11,987 Total Pizzas Sold**, yielding an **Average Order Value of 22.92** and **1.32 Pizzas Per Order**.
* **Revenue Drivers:** **The Thai Chicken Pizza** contributes the maximum revenue overall, with **The Spicy Italian Pizza** (35K) and **The Italian Supreme Pizza** (33K) also ranking among the top 5 earners.
* **Volume Leaders:** **The Classic Deluxe Pizza** generated both the highest overall total quantity sold and the highest total order count.
* **Underperforming Items:** **The Brie Carre Pizza** contributed the lowest total revenue (12K), lowest total quantity sold (0.5K), and lowest total order count (0.5K).
* **ClipKart Benchmark Data:** Secondary retail analytics show total revenue of **6.16M** across **1K orders**, led by Clothing (2.12M) and top sales cities like Chennai.

## Conclusion

This BI project successfully converts raw SQL order transactions into high-impact operational insights. By pinpointing top revenue generators like **The Thai Chicken Pizza** and volume leaders like **The Classic Deluxe Pizza**, business operators can focus promotional efforts on high-margin items while re-evaluating underperformers such as **The Brie Carre Pizza**.
